# Data Handling

## Purpose

Founder Chief of Staff is designed to be used with real company operations.

The repo itself ships with synthetic examples. Your working copy may contain sources, interviews, company notes, customer conversations, strategy, or credentials. Treat that material with the same care you would apply to any founder workspace.

## What Belongs In The Repo

- Framework docs.
- Operating principles.
- Agent contract.
- Evidence maturity ladder.
- Templates.
- Evaluation rubrics.
- Synthetic examples.
- Failure modes.
- Visual explanation of the operating loop.
- Scripts that check documentation quality and release safety.

## What Requires Care In Your Own Workspace

- Customer or user interview notes.
- Company research that gives you strategic advantage.
- Names, emails, intros, calendars, or access paths.
- Credentials, keys, tokens, and internal links.
- Paid pilot notes, pricing discussions, or unannounced plans.
- Any material covered by NDA or client obligations.

## Sharing Rule

Before sharing a repo, demo, or example:

1. Use synthetic examples where possible.
2. Remove credentials and tokens.
3. Remove names and access paths unless everyone involved expects publication.
4. Replace customer or interview material with abstracted examples.
5. Run the docs audit.
6. Run the repo safety check.

## Safety Test

Ask:

```text
Would I be comfortable with the people mentioned in this material reading it?
Would sharing this reduce my ability to act on the research?
Does this include credentials, access paths, or sensitive business context?
```

If yes, keep it in your local workspace or rewrite it as a synthetic example.
