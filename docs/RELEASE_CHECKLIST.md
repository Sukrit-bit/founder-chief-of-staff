## Positioning

Use this language:

```text
Founder Research OS is an open-source workspace for running AI-assisted startup discovery with memory, protocols, evals, and decision pressure.
```

Sharper version:

```text
Founder Research OS automates decision preparation while preserving founder judgment.
```

Do not pitch it as:

```text
AI notes for founders.
```

The claim is stronger:

```text
AI agents become more useful when the workspace gives them state, standards, and permission to improve the system.
```
