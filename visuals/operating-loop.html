Founder Research OS

From loose signals to startup decisions.

The system gives an AI agent memory, protocols, evals, and autonomy boundaries so founder research keeps moving toward evidence and judgment.

01 Input Article, company, call, transcript, hunch, or question.
02 Artifact Source note, case study, interview note, or theme update.
03 Pattern Repeated signal becomes a reusable pattern, not a vague memory.
04 Decision Queue Continue, narrow, experiment, park, pause, or kill.
05 Experiment Test the riskiest assumption with the smallest credible loop.
06 Updated State Dashboard, working state, and artifact index stay current.
