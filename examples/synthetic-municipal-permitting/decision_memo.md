# Decision Memo: Synthetic Municipal Permitting

Date: 2026-06-11
Evidence maturity: Problem candidate
Decision: Experiment, do not promote to thesis

## Decision

Run a narrow discovery experiment on permit resubmission workflows.

Do not promote this into a venture thesis yet.

## Why

The strongest synthetic signal is not general permit tracking. It is the action loop after a city sends comments back.

That workflow has a concrete work object:

- comment packet;
- missing-document list;
- responsibility map;
- resubmission package;
- applicant-facing update.

It also has a visible outcome: a cleaner resubmission.

## What Would Change The Decision

Continue if permit expediters say the workflow saves at least 30 minutes and reduces missed items.

Narrow if value appears only for one permit type or municipality.

Park if users see the output as useful but not worth paying for.

Kill if the work is already handled by a simple checklist or city portal.

## Next Evidence

1. Shadow one sample resubmission.
2. Interview three permit expediters.
3. Interview five small contractors.
4. Update the decision queue.
