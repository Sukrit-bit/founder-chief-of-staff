## Synthetic Setup

A founder is exploring whether small construction firms struggle with municipal permitting.

The founder has seen three fictional signals:

1. contractors complain that permit status is hard to track;
2. city portals differ by municipality;
3. expediters manually chase documents, comments, and resubmissions.

This is not validation. It is only enough to create a problem candidate and a discovery experiment.

## Decision

Current status: `Experiment, do not promote to venture thesis`.

Next evidence needed: 5 interviews with small contractors, 3 interviews with permit expediters, and 1 shadowed permit resubmission workflow.
